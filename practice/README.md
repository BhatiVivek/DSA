## Workflow

1. Copy the [problem template](../templates/problem.md) into the topic folder.
2. Record your initial reasoning before checking a solution.
3. Add the final approach, complexity, and mistakes.
4. Log the attempt above and choose a review date.
5. On review, solve it again from scratch and update the status.
